# Quickstart

1. Sales › Quotations › New quotation for Mountain Goats with "Bike Pump · Standard" × 2; Save and
   approve. Open it › Convert to order: the form shows the line at the quoted rate. Save:
   "SO-0002 saved." The quotation shows SO-0002 and no conversion buttons.
2. Sales › Deliveries › DN-0001 (2 boxes of Bike Pump, delivered) › Record return: 1, reason
   "Customer refused": "RTN-0001 recorded. 1 item back at Main Store." Stock of Bike Pump at Main
   Store is 42 (30 + 12). SO-0001 shows 1 dispatched, 3 left.
3. Returning 2 more is refused: "You can return at most 1 Box of Bike Pump · Standard".
4. SO-0001 › Create invoice (1 box), then DN-0001 › Record return is no longer offered.
