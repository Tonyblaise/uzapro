# Contract: UI

**Sales › Field sales** (one column on a phone):

- Van name (a picker when there is more than one), and a summary: Visits ("1 of 2"), Sales, Cash to
  hand in, M-Pesa, On credit.
- "Today's visits": a card per visit with the customer, phone, status and either "Start visit",
  the sale number, or the no-sale reason. Header action "Add visit".
- "On the van": product and quantity.

**Visit** (drawer): credit line (Available credit, Overdue), "Add item" (van stock only), lines with
Qty, Unit, Rate and remove, Total, "Paid by" (Cash, M-Pesa, Pay later (credit)), "Payment
reference (optional)", "Complete sale"; and "No sale" with a reason.
