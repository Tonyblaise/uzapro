# Quickstart: Approval rights and discount limits

1. People › Permissions › Edit role: Accountant shows credit and dispatch on, counts off, 20.
2. Edit role: Store keeper: turn on Sales Read and "Dispatch sales orders"; Save. View as Store
   keeper: Sales › Sales orders › SO-0001 offers Dispatch but not Edit, Create invoice or Cancel.
3. As the Cashier at the till, a 10% discount is refused: "Your role can give discounts up to 5%.
   This one is 10%." At 5% the sale completes.
4. Turn off the Accountant's "Approve credit over the limit": as the Accountant, a credit stop has
   no "Approve anyway".
5. Reload: back to the seed.
